Executive summary

Build a two-sided marketplace plus community where anyone can hire, collaborate with,
or watch performing and creative talent -- and where artists can form teams and run their
own shows. Positioning: "Hire, team up, and perform -- the operating system for live and
creative talent."

Most platforms today do only one job: booking (StarClinch, GigSalad), freelancing (Fiverr),
or ticketing (BookMyShow, District). Your edge is joining all three around one artist
identity: book  collaborate  perform  get rebooked, with payments, contracts and
reviews handled inside the app.

The winning wedge is not "everything for everyone" on day one. Start with one city, three
categories (DJs, singers/live bands, anchors/emcees), and private events (weddings,
birthdays, kitty and bachelor parties, corporate) where demand is frequent and prices
are mid-range. Teams, live shows and creator gigs layer on once bookings are flowing.

Gaps, and how you stand out

Gaps in your current idea (fix before building)

    Too broad on day one. "All artists, all events, live shows, teams" spreads thin supply
    across too many categories. Launch narrow, expand by data.
    No trust layer defined. Without escrow, verification and cancellation rules, hosts will
    keep using WhatsApp and artists will fear non-payment.
    No pricing logic. Hosts don't know what a DJ costs for 4 hours in Pune. You need visible
    price ranges and packages.
    Leakage. After the first booking, host and artist swap numbers and skip you. You need
    reasons to stay (see moat).
    Logistics ignored. Sound system, travel, stay, set duration, rider, power backup,
    permissions and noise curfews decide whether an event works.
    "Free live shows" has no business model. It is a growth and discovery feature;
    monetise via tips, tickets later, and sponsorships.
    Teams need money rules. Who gets paid what when a 5-member band is booked? Split
    payouts must be automatic.
    Safety for artists, especially women performing at private parties at night: verified
    hosts, check-in, SOS, and a host rating.

Gaps in the market you can own

 1. Mid and low-budget private events (5k-75k): kitty parties, birthdays, house parties,
    small weddings. StarClinch is pricey and agent-led; Instagram is risky.

 2. Instant, transparent booking: packages with fixed prices and real availability, not
    "request a quote and wait 2 days".

 3. Artist collaboration: finding a drummer for tonight's gig, forming a band, splitting pay.
    Nobody does this for live work in India.

 4. Tier 2/3 cities and regional artists: dhol, Sufi, qawwali, garba, bhajan, Punjabi, folk,
    anchors in Hindi and regional languages.

 5. Creator + event bundles: DJ + photographer + video editor + anchor as one package.

Your moat (why users stay)

    Artist OS, not just a listing: calendar, invoices, GST-ready payouts, contracts, team
    management, earnings dashboard, Instagram-linked portfolio. If artists run their
    business on your app, they bring their own clients to it.
    Protection only on-platform: escrow, no-show refund guarantee, backup artist
    replacement. Paying outside loses all of it.

End-to-end flows

A. Artist onboarding (target: live profile in under 10 minutes)

 1. Sign up with phone OTP or Google; pick "I'm an artist"
 2. Choose category and sub-skills (e.g. Singer  Bollywood, Sufi, Ghazal)
 3. Import from Instagram/YouTube: photos, reels, follower count
 4. Add 1-3 packages with price, duration, inclusions
 5. Set city, travel radius, languages, availability
 6. KYC (PAN/Aadhaar via DigiLocker) and bank/UPI for payouts
 7. Profile goes live as "New"; verified badge after KYC + first completed gig

B. Host booking (target: booked in under 5 minutes)

 1. Open app  choose event type (birthday, kitty party, club night...) or search
 2. Enter date, city, budget, guest count  see available artists with prices
 3. Compare, watch samples, chat (numbers hidden)
 4. Instant-book a package, or send a request and receive quotes
 5. Fill event brief (venue, timing, sound, song requests)
 6. Pay advance (e.g. 30-50%) into escrow; contract auto-generated
 7. Reminders to both sides at 7 days, 1 day and 3 hours
 8. Event day: artist checks in at venue via app
 9. After event: host confirms (or auto-confirms after 24-48 hrs if silent)  balance

     released to artist
10. Both leave reviews; host gets "rebook" and "book for next event" prompts
Branches: artist cancels  backup suggested + full refund option. Host cancels  refund
per policy. Dispute raised within 48 hrs  payout frozen, ops reviews chat, check-in data
and evidence.

C. Team / band flow

 1. Leader creates team, sets name, genre, combined packages
 2. Invites members via in-app search, phone, or invite link
 3. Members accept; leader sets default pay split (fixed or %)
 4. Booking comes in  members confirm availability for that date

D. Live show flow

1. Any user creates a show: title, artists/teams, venue or online, date, free/paid, capacity

3. Show is listed in city feed and on followers' timelines; RSVPs collected

2. Milestones with escrow per milestone
3. File delivery in app, up to N revisions
4. Approve  payment released

User acquisition and retention

Supply first, then demand, in one city. Aim for 300-500 verified artists in the launch city
before spending on host marketing.

Step 1: Pull artists (weeks 0-8)

    Manual onboarding: your team visits music schools, rehearsal studios, open mics,
    college fests, DJ academies; builds profiles for artists on the spot
    Instagram outreach: DM local DJs, singers, bands with a pre-built profile link ("Your
    profile is ready, claim it")
    Founding Artist program: first 500 artists get 0% commission for 6 months and a
    "Founding Artist" badge
    Guaranteed first gig: book some artists yourself for your own launch shows and
    partner events
    Referral: artist invites artist  both get commission credit
    Teams feature as a hook: bands join to manage members and splits even before they
    get bookings

    Event-anniversary and birthday reminders ("Book again for Riya's birthday?")
    Loyalty credits for hosts; lower commission for artists with high on-platform rebook

    Protection, reviews and contracts only apply to on-platform payments
    Mask contact info until booking is paid
    Make the artist dashboard so useful (calendar, invoices, earnings) that artists route
    their own clients through it

Growth loops

1. Booking  reels from the event  new hosts discover the artist  more bookings
2. Artist shares profile link in Instagram bio  fans and hosts land on the app
3. Live show  attendees follow artists  they host their own events later
4. Team leader invites members  members create their own profiles and teams
